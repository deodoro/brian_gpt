6.  True, False, and Explain : If the game is infinitely repeated, the equilibrium 
(Don't Cheat, Buy) may be sustainable even if a>5.  

TRUE.  If a>5, it never pays to Investigate.  But the customer can still find out about the store's 
honesty by buying without investigating and seeing  what happens.  He could then induce honest 
behavior with trigger strategies, for example.  Trigger strategies would work as long as 3/(1 -)5, 
where  is the store's discount factor.
