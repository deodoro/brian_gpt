9.  True, False, and Explain:   Repeated game theory provides a solid theoretical 
explanation for the widely -accepted view that collusion is more common than 
predation.  
 
FALSE.  Repeated game theory merely shows that in inf initely -repeated games, both collusion 
and predation MAY be sustainable.  It gives no particular reason to think that one is more 
sustainable than the other.  You need more details to understand why predation is less common 
than collusion.
