2.  Explain why the free -entry equilibrium with fixed costs is inefficient in a 
Cournot game.  Is this a realistic prediction?  Why or why not?  Explain how you 
would try to break out of this equil ibrium if you ran one of the firms.  
 
Take the simplest case where there is a fixed cost and zero MC.  Then obviously the cheapest 
way to make a quantity Q is to have one firm make it all.  The social cost is K, versus N*K for the 
free -entry equilibrium.  A ssuming a homogenous good, however, this prediction is highly 
unrealistic.  One firm could loudly announce that it is selling for one penny below the price 
reached by the Cournot auctioneer and steal the whole market.  Cournot oligopoly would then 
break do wn into contestable monopoly.
