1.  Suppose a million new immigrants enter the U.S., driving down the wages of 
low -skilled Americans by $1/hour.  
 
True, False, and Explain:  According to Landsburg ( Fair Play ), this change 
usually increases Kaldor -Hicks ef ficien cy, because the positive effect of 
population growth on innovation tends to outweigh the negative externality 
of falling wages.  
 
FALSE.  While Landsburg does discuss the R&D externalities of population growth, he explains 
that falling wages are not a n externality at all.  It is at worst a transfer from workers to employers, 
and if there is any demand elasticity the fall leads to additional surplus.
