4.  Consider the following 2 -player game:  
 
 Up Down  
Left  0,0  0,0  
Right  0,0  0,0  
 
True, False, and Explain:   This game has 4 PSNE but no MSNE.  
 
FALSE.  Th ere are 4 PSNE, but there is also an infinite number of MSNE.  Any probability mix 
leaves your opponent indifferent.  (Several students said that there was no reason  to randomize, 
but that misses the whole point of MSNE.  If neither play can do strictly do  better by changing, you  
have an equilibrium ).
