9.  Suppose firms in an industry have fixed costs and increasing  marginal costs.  
The industry demand curve lies stri ctly above firms' AC curves.  
 
True, False, and Explain:   Bertrand competition will allow only one firm to 
survive in this market, implying no productive inefficiency but some 
allocative inefficiency.  
 
FALSE.  With increasing MC, the AC curve eventually tur ns up – it will have a U -shape.  So if 
demand is high, multiple firms can definitely survive.  Ignoring divisibility problems, it is possible 
for multiple firms to survive in such an industry, setting P=AC=MC.
