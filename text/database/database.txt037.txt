1.  " The assumptions of the Arrow -Debreu model are sufficient but not necessary conditions for 
the efficiency of laissez -faire."  
 
Discuss this statement, using two specific examples from game theory to 
illustrate your point.   
 
Arrow -Debreu  assumes that firms have no control over prices.  But in the Bertrand oligopoly 
game, firms set prices, but can still reach the first -best outcome where P=MC.  Similarly, in the 
Ultimatum game, there  are no enforceable trades at all, and yet the game theoretic prediction 
($.01 for you, rest for me) is perfectly efficient.   In both cases, then, laissez -faire is fully efficient 
even though the A -D assumptions do not hold.
