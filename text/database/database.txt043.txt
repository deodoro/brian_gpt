4.  True, False, and Explain:   Landsburg's "Indifference Principle" ( The 
Armchair Economist ) is inconsistent with the concept of the MSNE.  
 
FALSE.  MSNE is actually a special case of Landsburg's Indiffere nce Principle.  The Indifference 
Principle states that in equilibrium, people will be indifferent between all of their choices.  If one 
were better than the other, everyone would do it.  And this is precisely what happens in a MSNE: 
ever agent is indiffere nt between every choice.
