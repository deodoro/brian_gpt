5.  Consider the following 2 -player game:  
 
 Up Down  
Left  10,0  10,0  
Right  0,10  0,10 
 
True, False, and Explain:   This game has no  PSNE and one  MSNE.  
 
FALSE.  Since Left is strictly dominant for Player 1, and Up and Down have equa l payoffs for 
Player 2 , there are two PSNE: (Left, Up), and (Left, Down).  But there are also infinitely many 
MSNE, where Player 1 plays L with p=1, and Player 2 plays Up with any  probability.
