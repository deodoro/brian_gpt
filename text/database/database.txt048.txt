9.  Suppose the demand curve for a contestable monopo ly crosses the AC curve 
at more than one point.  
 
True, False, and Explain:   There are multiple equilibria.  
 
FALSE.  Under contestable monopoly, P=AC, but only the higher quantity/lower price intersection 
is an equilibrium.  At the higher intersection, even  a monopolist that DID NOT face potential 
competition would want to cut price somewhat to earn additional profits.  If you do face potential 
competition, you would have to cut your price all the way down to the lowest intersection  of 
demand and AC  to preve nt entry.
