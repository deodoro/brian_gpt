5.  True, False, and Explain:   Played simultaneously, this  game has two 
SGPPSNE.  Played sequentially (with Player 1 moving first), this  game has 
two PSNE, but only one is SGP.  
 
TRUE.  The simultaneous game only has one subgame, so both (L, L) and (R,R) are SGPNE.  
The sequential gam e still has two PSNE: Player 1 will play L if he believes Player 2 will play L, and 
R if he believes Player 2 will play R.  However, once Player 1 plays L, Player 2 would definitely 
want to play L too, so by backw ards induction, only (L,L) is SGP.
