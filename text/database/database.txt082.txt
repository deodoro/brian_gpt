1.  Suppose your P(more abortions cause less crime)=. 4, P(Levitt’s research  
results | more abortions cause less crime)=.6, and P(more abortions cause less 
crime | Levitt’s research results)=.8 .   
 
True, False, and Explain :  If you satisfy Bayes’ Rule, your P(Levitt’s research  
results | more abortions don’t  cause less crime) must be .1. 
 
TRUE.  Let A=more abortions cause less crime, and B=Levitt’s research results, then apply 
Bayes’ Rule and solve for P(B|~A):   
 
.8=.6*.4/( .6*.4+P(B|~A)*.6)  
 
.8=.24/(.24+P(B|~A)*.6)  
 
so P(B|~A)=.1.
