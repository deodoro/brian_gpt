5.  True, False, and Explain:  Kreps’  monopolist engages in “limit pricing.”  
 
FALSE.  The notes explain that “limit pricing” is when the lowest -cost firm sets its price just below 
the costs of the second -lowest -cost firm.  In Kreps’ example, in contrast, firms have equal costs.  
What Kreps’ monopolist is trying to do is take advanta ge of its first -mover advantage  to make 
output so high that an entrant would be unable to cover its costs.
