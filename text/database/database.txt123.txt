5.  True, False, and Explain:   Bertrand competition between identical firms 
with fixed costs yields a second -best efficient outcome.  
 
TRUE.  The first -best outcome  is when one firm produces at MC, avoiding any allocative 
inefficiency from P>MC (the Harberger triangle).  But in Bertrand competition with fixed costs, 
competition leads one  firm to drop out, and the other to set P=AC – a standard second -best result.  
At lower prices, firms would lose money and exit.  Bertrand competition will NOT lead to wasteful 
duplication of fixed costs; if it did, either firm could profit by slightly cutting its price and stealing all 
its competitors’ customers.
