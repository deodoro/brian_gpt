6.  Consider the f ollowing one -shot game : 
 Attack  Submit  Defend  
Attack  -10, -10 5,-3 -20, -5 
Submit  -3,5 0,0  0,-1 
Defend  -5,-20 -1,0  -1,-1 
 
True, False, and Explain:   There is a MSNE, but no PSNE.  
 
FALSE.  Defend is strictly dominated by Submit for both players, so this g ame simplifies to a 
standard Hawk/Dove game with the four upper -left cells.  In such games, there are two PSNE – 
(Attack, Submit) and (Submit, Attack) in this case, plus a MSNE.
