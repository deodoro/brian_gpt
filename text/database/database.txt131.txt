2. True, False, and E xplain:   This game has one PSNE if the players play 
sequentially, but three PSNE if the players play simultaneously.  

FALSE.  For the simultaneous game, (Comics, Comics, Comics), (Chess, Chess, Chess), and 
(Opera, Opera, Opera) are all PSNE equilibria.  Bu t so is every situation where each players plays 
a different  strategy: e.g. (Comics, Chess, Opera).  This gives everyone zero payoffs, but no 
individual can increase his payoff by switching.  For the sequential game , there is only one 
subgame perfect  PSNE,  where the first mover picks the strategy that he most prefers everyone to 
play – Comics for Bryan, Chess for Tyler or John.  But every PSNE in the simultaneous game 
remains a PSNE in the sequential game.
