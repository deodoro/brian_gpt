6.  Consider the following one -shot game : 
 Attack  Submit  
Attack  -1, -1000  100,1  
Submit  1,100  50,50  
 
True, False, and Explain:   There are two PSNE and one MSNE, but only 
(Attack, Submit) is focal.  
 
TRUE .  This is just the Hawk -Dove game, so (Attack, Submit) and (Submit, Attack) are the two 
PSNE, and there is also a MSNE.  But given the fact that (Attack, Attack) is extremely  bad for 
player 2, (Attack, Submit) is a strong focal point: Both players will lo ok at it and think “Player 2 
won’t want to risk attacking.”
