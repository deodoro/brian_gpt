3. Consider the following variant on the Ultimatum Game:  
 
  Player 2  Player 
1  Accept  Reject  
t (10 -t),t 0, Z 
 
Z=0 if t≥5, but Z=1 if t<5.  
 
True,  False, and Explain:  There are an infinite number of PSNE, but only one 
SGPNE.  
 
TRUE.  This is just like the Ultimatum Game in the notes, except that the unique SGPNE is now 
for Player 1 to offer 1 (I also accepted 1.01 and 1+ ε), because Player 2 strictly prefers to Reject 
offers less than 1.  As i n the Ultimatum Game in the notes, there are an infinite number of PSNE, 
because IF Player 1 expects Player 2 to Reject any offer less than X, Player 1’s optimal response 
is to offer X.  In this variant, X can be any number from 1 -10.
