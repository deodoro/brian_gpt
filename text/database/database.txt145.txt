1. Caplan (1999, 2001) defends the unrestricted use of subject ive probability 
theory in economics.  What is the best  objection to his position ?  State your case 
as forcefully as possible.   
 
There were several good answers to this question.  One was that most people assign discrete 
probability categories (certain, hi ghly likely, somewhat likely, 50/50, somewhat unlikely, highly 
unlikely, impossible) rather  than 0 -1 numbers, and that these categories predict their behavior 
better than a continuous model.  Another was that most people don’t know Bayes’ Rule, and 
therefo re frequently violate subjective probability theory on a very deep level.
