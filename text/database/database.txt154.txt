6.  Suppose N equally efficient firms lobby the government for a monopoly 
privilege.  The firms never collude.  
True, False, and Explain:   Bertrand -style  lobbying  will lead to a larger  
Tullock rectangle than Cournot -styl e lobbying  would.  
TRUE.  Note that the question specifies Bertrand - or Cournot -style LOBBYING.  The natural 
interpretation is that with Bertrand -style lobbying, whichever firms lobbies the MOST wins the 
monopoly for sure, whereas with Cournot -style lobbyin g, your chance of winning the privilege  
depends on your lobbying as a fraction of TOTAL lobbying.  By the usual logic, Bertrand  lobbying 
leads one firm to set  its lobbying equal to the total value of the monopoly, and all rivals to give up.  
Cournot lobbyi ng, in contrast, leads all firms to lobby somewhat, but not enough to wipe out all the 
profits (i.e., to get full rent dissipation).
