1. Suppose all the assumptions of the First Welfare Theorem are true.  
 
True, False, and Explain:   Markets are Pareto and  Kaldor -Hicks efficient.  
 
TRUE.  The First Wel fare Theorem assumes no externalities, perfect information, and an 
exogenous price vector, and proves markets are Pareto efficient.  Given these assumptions, 
moreover, people sell ANYTHING they possess that ANYONE else values more highly  than they 
do, so m arkets maximize total surplus and are therefore K -H efficient . 
 
Most students  answered FALSE, citing the notes on the fact that Pareto efficiency does not imply 
K-H efficiency.  This is true in the real world, but the assumptions of the First Welfare Theor em 
are so unrealistic that both Pareto and K -H efficiency follow !
