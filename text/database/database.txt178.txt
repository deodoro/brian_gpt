1. Suppose a million new immigrants enter the U.S., driving down the wages of 
low -skilled Americans by $1/hour.  
 
True, False, and Explain:  According to Landsburg ( Fair Play ), this change 
usually increases Kaldor -Hicks effic ien cy, because the positive 
externalities of  population growth hugely  outweigh the negati ve externality 
of falling wages for natives.  
 
FALSE.  Landsburg says that falling wages are not  a negative externality at all; instead, they’re a 
TRANSFER to capitalis ts.  As long as there is any demand elasticity, moreover, total surplus 
expands at the lower wage, so Kaldor -Hicks efficiency automatically increases.  Landsburg 
discuses the positive externalities of popula tion growth elsewhere, but they’re  not relevant  here.
