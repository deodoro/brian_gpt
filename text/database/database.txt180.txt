3.  Consider t he following 2 -player game:  
 Up Down  
Left  0,0  0,0  
Right  0,0  0,0  
 
True, False, and Explain:   This game has 4 PSNE but no MSNE.  
 
FALSE.  The game has 4 PSNE, but also an infinite number of MSNE.  Since all payoffs are zero, 
EVERY combination of probabilit ies leaves everyone indifferent, so they’re all MSNEs.
