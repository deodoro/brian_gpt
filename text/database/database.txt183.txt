6.  True, False, and Explain:   The War/Peace game confirms the view that the 
invention of atomic weapons reduced  the risks of war and  human 
extinction.   
FALSE.  The PSNE of the War/Peace game remains (Peace, War) and (War, Peace) no matter 
how bad the (War, War) payoffs become .  But for the MSNE, the frequency of playing War falls as 
the (War, War) payoffs fall, and the frequency of the (War, War) result falls quadratically.  
However, the risk of actual war only declines because at omic weapons make actual war so 
terrible IF it occurs!  With more primitive weapons, it is unclear if human extinction was even a 
possible outcome of war.  Now, it may be.
