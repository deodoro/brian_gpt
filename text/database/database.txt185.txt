2. Suppose artificial intelligence researchers competitively  produce  a zero MC 
perfect substitute for ALL human labor  (but not other inputs) .  Use ge neral 
equilibrium theory to predict the effects on output, wages, profit, and overall 
human  welfare . 
 
This innovation effectively makes one input – labor – free.  Wages therefore fall to 0 – why pay 
humans a positive wage when an AI will do an equally good  job for free?   Since infinite labor is 
now available for no cost, output soars.  Profits, however, remain zero in equilibrium; with 
competition, all the benefits get passed on to the remaining scarce resources of capital and land.  
Since all production i s consumed by humans, overall human welfare rise s, though workers who 
own very little capital and land  will be worse off.
