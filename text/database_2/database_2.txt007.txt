7. After several decades, the government finally eliminates sugar subsidies in 2005. The sugar industry is thrown into a severe crisis and many plantations go bankrupt. 

True, False, and Explain: This is strong evidence that sugar executives did not have rational expectations about government sugar policy.

False. *week 8 RE definition from notes  -  essentially, while you expect people to reasonably estimate the outcomes of certain events, doesn't mean these estimates are perfect. 

RE means that people's subjective probability = the objective probability. But it does not rule out error or suggest that information or cognition is free. So, while someone with RE may be expected to reasonably estimate the outcome of future events, it does not preclude error. The experts may not have known that the subsidies were going to end.
