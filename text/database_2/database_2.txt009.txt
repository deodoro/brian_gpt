9. In a "psychologist/engineer" problem, the base rate is 70% engineers, 30% lawyers. Fred's P(having a pocket protector| a person is an engineer)=.5, and P(a person is an engineer|having a pocket protector)=.8. If the base rate is changed to 30% engineers, 70% lawyers, Fred's P(a person is an engineer|having a pocket protector) falls to .3.
 
True, False, and Explain: Fred does NOT suffer from representativeness bias.

True. *Week 11 notes. Fred adjusts for base rates. 

Fred does not suffer from representativeness bias because his P(a person is an engineer|having a pocket protector) adjust for a change in the base rate. 

Bayes' Rule: P(A|B)=(P(B|A)P(A))/(P(B|A)P(A)+P(B|~A)P(~A))
E-30% L-70%
P(A|B)=(0.5(0.3))/((0.5)(0.3)+0.5(0.7))=0.15/0.5=0.3
Fred's P(a person is an engineer|having a pocket protector)=0.3, thus Fred does not suffer from representativeness bias.
