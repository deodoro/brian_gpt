10. More educated workers tend to enjoy their work more and are less likely to be unemployed. 
 
True, False, and Explain: BOTH of these factors bias standard estimates of the return to education downwards.

False. Standard estimates of the return to education only concern wages. Omitting utility of work biases the estimate upward -- the true estimate of education and income should be lower[6][7]. More educated workers are less likely to be unemployed, which means they're more likely to earn more income over their lifetime.
