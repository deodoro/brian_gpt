4.True, False, and Explain: The outcome of the game is more efficient if costs are sunk.
 
 True. That's the only game where you get enter, enter and price falls to MC.[11][12] The outcome enter, enter doesn't occur when costs are NOT sunk. 
 
Under monopoly, total surplus is (Pm+ CSmon) with probability 1. -  So when is contestability welfare-dominated by simple monopoly? -  When the expected surplus under contestability is less than (Pm + CSmon):
 
 - (-2a + CSmax) + 2 (Pm+ CSmon) <  - (Pm+ CSmon)
 
Thus, suppose that Pm=a. -  Then contestability is welfare-dominated if:
 
.25* (-2a + CSmax) + .25* (a + CSmon) < (a + CSmon)
 
which reduces to:
 
a > CSmax  - - -  3*CSmon
 
i.e., monopoly welfare dominates if the fixed costs exceeds the consumer surplus under competition minus three times the consumer surplus under monopoly.
