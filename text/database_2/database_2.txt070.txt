9. True, False, and Explain: If the efficient markets hypothesis is correct, then the marginal private cost of irrational investing can easily be zero.

True. Under the efficient markets hypothesis, asset prices changes should be completely unpredictable--asset prices should follow a random walk. If this is true, then there is no payoff to rational investing--if you indulge your own irrational preferences, you are likely to do as well as anyone else. A more viable strategy would be to purchase index funds and hedge against variance.
