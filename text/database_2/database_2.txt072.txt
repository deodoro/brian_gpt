2. True, False, and Explain: In The Armchair Economist, Landsburg defends the market as a Darwinian mechanism that weeds out inefficiency.

False. Landsburg says that the analogy of markets being like Darwinian evolution fails because the Darwinian mechanism doesn't ensure efficient outcomes - male birds of paradise have ridiculously long tails (lemme smash) that hinder them and it would benefit all of them to agree to not have long tails, so Darwinian evolution leads to an inefficient outcome. It is necessary to have prices so that competition can lead to efficient outcomes.[22]
