5. True, False, and Explain: Asymmetric information problems do not require irrationality, but irrationality makes asymmetric information problems more severe.

False. It could be the case that irrationality may make asymmetric information problems LESS severe. If I buy car insurance, and instead drive more safely instead of taking on more risks, I am making things better for other people in the insurance market--I am not behaving in the way a rational person in a situation of moral hazard would and not fully externalizing costs onto others.
