8. True, False, and Explain: There is no change in current U.S. policy that would do much to mitigate moral hazard problems. 

False. Policy changes that allowed for more punitive punishments in contracts could reduce moral hazard. Allowing companies to charge different premiums for riskier insurance clients could also help. Finally, the government could stop subsidizing certain kinds of behavior, such as bad driving by buying insurance for poor drivers  or education for students.
