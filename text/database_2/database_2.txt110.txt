1. Two players are deciding whether to contribute to a public good.  The public good is discrete: it is produced at the optimal level so long as one person contributes.  If the public good is produced, everyone gets a benefit of 3 if they didn't contribute, and 2 if they did.  If no one contributes, everyone gets 0.  
 
True, False, and Explain:  This is a Prisoners' Dilemma, not a Hawk-Dove game.
 
False. Like in a Hawk-Dove Game, there are two PSNE, both of which require that one person contributes while the other doesn't. Like in the hawk-dove games we saw in class, two PSNEs exist--both of which require one player chooses peace and the other player chooses war. Unlike the Hawk-Dove games we saw in class where peace, peace is a first best outcome, the contribute, contribute is not a first-best solution in the public goods provision game. This is not a prisoner's dilemma--a prisoner's dilemma has one PSNE, which is the first-best outcome.
