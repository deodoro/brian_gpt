All situations are Pareto efficient because it is never possible to compensate the inevitable losers from a change due to transactions costs; I will therefore focus on Kaldor-Hicks efficiency, the maximization of the dollar value of social resources.

The government could maximize Kaldor-Hicks efficiency by having individuals bid for how they would like the money to be spent. By doing this, the government can ensure that the billion dollars will go towards the most highly valued use. (but if people can bid up to 1 billion dollars for a project, why won't they undertake the project themselves without the government?)

The goal under a utilitarian system is to maximize the total "happiness" or pleasure in a society. Usually, one thinks that this can be done by redistributing the billion dollars to those who are the poorest and have a higher marginal utility per dollar they receive. However, "utility monsters" may well exist, so the  government may maximize utility by spending it all on a project that one individual gains nearly infinite utility from, rather [33][34][35][36]than being concerned about everyone else.
