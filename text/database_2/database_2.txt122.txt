False, while you do not need communication to reach a PSNE in a Coordination game, it is not like a PD game. Specifically, a PD has only one PSNE while a Coordination game has two. In a coordination game, two players can coordinate using focal points like culture and location. However, communication increases the likelihood  that the players will reach a PSNE, and one with the higher valued payoff. In a PD game, there is only one PSNE: defect, defect. Communication is not necessary for players to reach this outcome; instead, it may move players away from it.
