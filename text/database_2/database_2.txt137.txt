2. Both the human capital and signaling models of education suggest that online education will expand more quickly if Ôearly-adopters' of online education are relatively strong students."  Do you agree?  Carefully explain your reasoning.
 
To answer this question, we would need to know more about the structure of the online education. For the human capital model, it depends on if the online education resources allow students to work together. The presence of other strong students may serve to help you build better human capital, as you have the opportunity to form study groups or learn from other talented students. If the structure does not allow you to interact with smarter students but instead has you working alone, we should not expect the early adopters being strong students to have any effects. For the signaling model, the online education must be "costly" enough to serve as an effective signal - there must be some barriers to completing. If this is the case, then talented students would increase the adoption of online education - their presences would increase the strength of the signal (talented students come from this program.) If this is true, we would expect others to rapidly adopt the model so as to signal to employers they are higher ability, and thus deserving of higher wages.
